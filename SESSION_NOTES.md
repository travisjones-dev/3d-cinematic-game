### Skills pass (2026-07-28) — two new skills, and THREE corrections to existing ones
Distilled from the 2026-07-27 playtest round. The corrections matter more than the additions: **three existing skills contained guidance that actively caused today's bugs.**
- ❌→✅ **`godot-scene-authoring`** said the 9 basis floats are "x-axis, y-axis, z-axis **columns**". They are **ROWS** — the X axis is the *first element of each triple* `(a, d, g)`, matching Godot's own 9-float `Basis()` constructor. Reading them as columns gives the transpose, i.e. the same rotation with the opposite sign, which is exactly how the steed ended up backwards. Symmetric rotations (45° tilt, 180° flip) look identical under both readings, so the error hides until an asymmetric case. Also added: a 180° flip is safe under either reading, and a "measure, don't derive" procedure.
- ❌→✅ **`godot-zone-building`** said to "leave a **gap** where the exit is so the player can leave". That is wrong — exits are press-E, so no physical opening is needed, and the gap is what let the player walk off the village's north edge. Barriers must be **continuous**.
- ❌→✅ **`godot-dialogic-integration`** claimed a leading `"` is misparsed. **It is not** (verified by dumping parsed events on alpha-19). The real rule is narrower and was found by probe: **a colon preceded by a single unbroken token at line start becomes a speaker name** — `"Wait: listen."` yields a phantom character `"Wait`, while `She said: nothing at all.` is fine because `She said` contains a space. Since quoted speech is now the house style, the live hazard is a colon right after the opening quoted word.
- Added to **`godot-gut-headless-testing`**: a test proves nothing until you've watched it fail (the `disabled = true` trick, with the real failure numbers); **GUT fails a test on ANY engine error**, including the benign dummy-renderer `Parameter "material" is null` — so validate authored scene data via `SceneState` instead of instantiating; assert event *types and text*, not just counts; and `global_position` needs `await get_tree().process_frame` (a bare `SceneTree` script reports all-zeros).
- Added to **`godot-multisegment-terrain`**: headroom maths for placing anything under the spiral — step *n* at angle `(5 + 10n)°`, surface `y ≈ 0.0625 + 0.125n`, underside ≈ 0.15 below, tread spans r 2.5..3.5 tangentially.
- **Method note:** while writing the Dialogic correction I checked today's own quotation work against the old warning and probed the parser rather than trusting either. The probe created a temp `.dtl`, which **Dialogic auto-registered in `project.godot`'s `dtl_directory`** — a phantom entry that had to be removed along with the file. Recorded in the skill.
