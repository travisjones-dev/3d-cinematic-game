# Zone 01 — Home Village

> Story purpose (Zone Design Law, CLAUDE.md). Written by the human (Travis), 2026-07-14.
> No geometry gets built until this paragraph exists. This is the tiebreaker for every layout decision in this zone.

## Story purpose (author's words)

Warm and full of life. Feels like home. Surrounded by welcoming, warm, smiling faces, proud to see
how far our young knight has come. This village should make the player feel calm and relaxed, and
protective of its inhabitants because it's home. Then its inevitable destruction will be more
devastating. I picture an elder of the village engaging with the player, smiling at him and sharing
wisdom.

## Environmental-storytelling setups (Zone Design Law requires 3+)
1. **A wedding being prepared in the square** — flowers, hung banners; a future that won't happen.
2. **The stables** — visit the young knight's steed and talk with the stout, strong, stocky blacksmith.
3. **The abandoned guard tower** — flirt with your love among cracked, age-worn stone.

## Secret (Zone Design Law requires 1+)
Behind the mill: a carved heart with the player's and the love interest's initials, visible only if
you wander off the main path.

## Cast introduced here (for the writing pass / Dialogic characters)
- The **elder** (wisdom, warmth — the voice of home)
- The **blacksmith** (stout, strong, stocky)
- The **love interest** (flirtation at the guard tower; escapes the raid, revealed later)
- The **best friend** (fellow young knight; travels out with you — dies at the shore)
