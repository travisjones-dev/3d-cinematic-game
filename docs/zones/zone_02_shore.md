# Zone 02 — The Shore

> flag for a human writing pass / approval.

## Story purpose (draft)

The cold inverse of the village. Where the village was warm, close, and full of life, the shore is
open, grey, and empty — endless water swallowed by mist. Its whole job is to be the stage for the
slice's one cinematic moment: the player and their best friend arrive out of the woods, the boats
resolve from the fog, and everything the village promised is revoked in a few seconds. It is not an
explorable zone; it is a threshold you cross once. The player arrives with control, has a breath to
register the emptiness, then the cutscene takes over: the camera pushes past the two figures out
over the water, the raider hulls emerge, and the friend's sword slips from his hand. If the player
promised to "stay close" back in the village, the moment lands harder — the promise is named as it
breaks.

## Environmental / mood
- Cold, desaturated, heavy mist (fog). Dim, low, cool light — the opposite of the village's gold.
- A wide flat shore, water stretching to -Z, dark boat shapes looming in the fog.

## The cinematic beat (from GAME_PITCH.md)
Boats emerge from the mist → friend drops his sword → player is pulled into a run. Skippable,
fires once, save-safe (completion flag `act1/shore_cutscene_seen`). Reflects `act1/promised_to_protect`.
