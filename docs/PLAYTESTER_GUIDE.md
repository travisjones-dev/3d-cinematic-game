# Playtest guide

Thank you for trying this. It takes about **10 minutes**. You need a **Windows PC**. There is nothing
to install and it will not change anything on your computer.

---

## Step 1 — Download the file

You should have a file called **`vertical-slice-playtest.zip`** (about 40 MB).

## Step 2 — Unzip it first (please do not skip this)

Right-click the zip file and choose **Extract All…**, then **Extract**. Somewhere easy like your
Desktop is fine.

> Running the game from *inside* the zip without extracting it can fail or behave oddly. Extract it
> properly first.

## Step 3 — Open the game

Go into the folder you just extracted and double-click **`vertical-slice.exe`**.

## Step 4 — Windows will try to stop you. This is expected.

You will probably see a blue box saying **"Windows protected your PC."**

1. Click **More info**
2. Click **Run anyway**

**This is normal and the game is safe.** Windows shows this warning for any program that has not been
through a paid certification process, which this early test build has not. If your antivirus
quarantines it, choose to allow it.

The game opens in a window and you will be standing in a village.

---

## Before you play: this is an early build

This is a **story test, not a look test.** The real art has not been made yet —
people are capsules, buildings are boxes, and nearly everything you see is a simple
placeholder shape standing in for the real thing.

**So a lot of it will look plain, unfinished, or frankly silly. That is expected and
already known.** What is being tested is whether the world and the story pull you
in — not whether it looks good yet.

## Controls

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Look around | **Move the mouse** |
| Walk | **W A S D** |
| Run | Hold **Shift** |
| Talk / examine something | **E** when a prompt appears on screen |
| Continue a conversation | **Space** or **left-click** |
| Choose a reply | **Click** the option you want |
| Pause, save, or quit | **Esc** |

**Your mouse cursor will disappear when you start playing.** That is on purpose so the mouse can move
the camera. It comes back on its own during conversations, and whenever you press **Esc**.

---

## What to do

Just wander and be nosy. Walk around the village, talk to anyone you come across, and look at
anything that gives you a prompt. Follow whatever seems interesting.

**If you get stuck or are not sure where to go:** find your friend at the edge of the village and talk
to him. He will tell you where to go next.

To finish, press **Esc** and choose **Quit** — or close the window. You can stop whenever you like,
even if you do not reach the end.

## What would really help to hear back

There is a short **anonymous** feedback form. It asks for no name, and nothing is
sent or saved anywhere — at the end it just formats your answers as text on your own
screen for you to copy and send back however is easiest.

> ### 👉 https://claude.ai/code/artifact/c3d640b1-3b27-437a-b12b-1b6ca15dc5e2

The single most useful thing on it, by a wide margin:

> ### Any question about this world that came into your head — even one you think is silly.
> **Please jot these down as they occur to you, while you are playing.** They are easy to
> forget afterwards, and they matter more than bug reports.

The form also asks where you got lost, whether you wanted to know what happens next,
and whether anything seemed broken. Filling it in is ideal because it stays anonymous,
but if you would rather just talk it through, that is fine too — the answers matter
more than the format.

Blunt feedback is more useful than kind feedback. "I had no idea where to go" is a gift.

## If something goes wrong

**It will not open at all, or closes immediately.**
Usually means your graphics drivers are older than the game expects. Message me with your PC details
rather than fighting it — I can send you a version that works on more machines. (It will not look
quite right, so if you do end up using that one, please tell me, because it changes what your
feedback about the visuals means.)

**The screen is black, or I am stuck in the scenery.**
Press **Esc** and quit, then reopen it. Please tell me where it happened.

**My mouse cursor vanished.**
Normal — press **Esc** to get it back.

**I cannot talk to a person or object.**
Stand a bit closer and wait for the prompt text to appear on screen, then press **E**. If a prompt
never appears when you feel it should, that is a bug worth telling me about.

**It says something about a virus or is blocked from downloading.**
See Step 4 above. Unsigned test builds trip these warnings routinely. If your browser refuses to
download it at all, tell me and I will send it another way.
